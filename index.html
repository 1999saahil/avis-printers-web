Got Questions? We Have Answers

Everything you need to know about placing bulk printing orders, GeM portal execution, and GST tax invoicing.

1. How do I place an order via WhatsApp or GeM Portal?

You can either fill out our Instant Quote Form on this website (which formats and opens WhatsApp directly to +91 8285462728) or share your purchase order on our GeM Portal registered account. Senior print managers will immediately confirm artwork files and timeline.

2. Do you issue GST Tax Invoices for corporate & institutional orders?

Yes! Avis Printers is a fully registered firm with an official GST Number issued in 2010. Every commercial and government order includes a valid 100% tax compliant GST invoice.

3. What is the turnaround time for urgent express orders?

Standard orders are completed within 2 to 4 business days. For urgent events, conferences, or launch campaigns, we provide 24 to 48-Hour Express Dispatch for roll-up standees, banners, and flyers.

4. Can Avis Printers assist us with artwork design and layout?

Absolutely! Whether you already have print-ready vector files or need our in-house creative team to design custom layouts, logo branding, and die-cuts from scratch, we have dedicated design experts.

5. What are the minimum order quantities (MOQ)?

We cater to both single-unit custom prototypes (like 1 standee or 1 custom acrylic plate) and 100,000+ bulk promotional runs for corporate and government campaigns.
